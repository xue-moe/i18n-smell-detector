# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming and respectful space for everyone.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate with empathy, patience, and professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially when discussing bugs or design tradeoffs.
- Keep examples neutral and avoid targeting real people or private projects.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and priorities.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after a maintainer asks to move on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users who violate this code of conduct.

If you see harmful behavior, please contact the maintainer privately.
